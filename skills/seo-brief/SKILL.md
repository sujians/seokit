# B2B Technical and Procurement Content Brief

1. **Read the profile and cluster.** Reuse a validated profile or call `seokit_load_profile({})`. Establish the topic, market, language, reader role, and primary search task. Use the matching page and links in `clusters/*-clusters.yaml`. Continue in generic mode when the profile is unusable. Choose create or update mode from the target URL, cluster, and request.
2. **Research and inspect the leading results.** Follow [SERP, gap, and terminology research](references/serp-gap-and-research.md). Use `seokit_fetch_page` and `seokit_parse_seo` on up to five relevant organic pages, aiming for at least three when available. Use the actual formats and coverage to identify what readers still need. In update mode, identify sections to retain, strengthen, or add.
3. **Put the search task first.** Use the [page templates](references/page-type-templates.md). For informational topics such as standards references, process principles, and selection basics, devote at least 80% of the article to the core technical content. Keep all conversion content at or below 20%, preferably 10–15%, including DFM handoff, procurement submission checklists, service descriptions, and inquiry CTAs. Lead procurement guides, product pages, and selection comparisons with objective specifications and decision criteria. Keep supplier workflows concise. Plan depth from the core questions and examples, especially when the SERP is weak.
4. **Build the brief.** Use the [brief output template](references/brief-output-template.md) to hand off the outline, metadata, links, and Schema.org recommendations. Distinguish established industry knowledge with worked examples, specific public facts that need authoritative verification, and factory claims that require profile or user evidence. Use plain section names and short source notes. Add cross-references only where they make the handoff clearer.
5. **Check, save, and hand off.** Run the template's delivery checklist, then save `drafts/<slug>-brief.md` and read it back. Share the path and open evidence. Give `seo-write` the saved brief for drafting and `seo-schema` the type recommendations and their prerequisites when markup is requested. Keep the saved brief available even if a downstream draft is blocked.
