## Conversions and specification fidelity

| Quantity | Metric to U.S. customary | U.S. customary to metric |
|---|---|---|
| Length or length tolerance | in = mm ÷ 25.4 | mm = in × 25.4 |
| Mass | lb = kg ÷ 0.45359237 | kg = lb × 0.45359237 |
| Temperature reading | °F = °C × 9/5 + 32 | °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9 |
| Temperature difference or tolerance | Δ°F = Δ°C × 9/5 | Δ°C = Δ°F × 5/9 |

- Convert each dimension, endpoint, and tolerance individually, preserving order, sign, and range. Mark rounded additions as approximate and retain the source value, e.g. `635 mm (25 in)` or `10 kg (approx. 22 lb)`. Use the original specification for acceptance. Preserve original significant figures and display enough precision to distinguish endpoints and tolerances.
- Preserve unclear units and variables pending verification. Retain threads, sheet gauge, pipe sizes, material grades, and electrical ratings with their standards and models. Use a sourced cross-reference for nominal values. Verify equivalence before treating similar grades, supply conditions, or component ratings as interchangeable.
- Localize number separators in prose. Preserve characters in model, drawing, and certification numbers, revisions, and date identifiers. Retain ambiguous dates and currencies for review.
